Why this is the whole booking flow: ＋ Нов час → month picker (which day) → the day opens in the ordinary schedule screen → tap a free slot (which time) → form with day + time already filled → Запази leads to the share screen (epic 6). Step 2 is not a special picker — it's the same Днес screen, so reading a day and booking into it are the same gesture. Near-term bookings skip step 1 entirely: just tap a slot on today's view.

Good to lock as the direction? Then the last open piece is where the provider ↔ client mode switch lives.
