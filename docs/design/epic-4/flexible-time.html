The ◷ друг час chip sits after the quick slots — tapped here, it opens the picker at the gap start. Choose 11:15 → straight to the form, day + time filled.

How flexible time works:
  • Quick slots stay for the common case — one tap, no thinking.
  • ◷ друг час opens a minute picker bounded to the free window (10:30–12:00 here), so an odd time like 11:15 is easy and a clash is impossible. Default step 5 min (fine enough for a salon; configurable).
  • It's the same picker the form's “Промени” uses — so whether you set the time from the slot or fix it later on the form, it behaves identically. One way to pick a time, two doors to it.

Sound right? Or tweak — minute step (5 vs to-the-minute), or make every slot a range that opens the picker. Then the last piece is the mode switch placement.
